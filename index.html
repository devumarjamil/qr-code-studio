QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Everything you need to know.

Is QR AI Studio free to use? ＋

Yes. You can generate, customize, download, and save QR codes locally without creating an account.

Does my link get uploaded? ＋

No. QR generation happens in your browser. Your saved history is stored in local browser storage on your device.

Can I use these QR codes commercially? ＋

Absolutely. Downloaded QR codes are yours to use on menus, packaging, marketing materials, events, and more.
